# Deferred original Outcome loading

This isolated extension of [Work-only inference](work-only-inference.md) connects
the Lean comparison to actual discovery. It is not product adoption or a faster
hook. Sources, link inputs, receipts and failed attempts are pinned in
[the review](candidates/deferred-work-review.json).

## Preserve Work consequences

Discovery retains every raw-admitted Work root in original ordinal order, even
when its Outcome is deferred. Roots still participate in topology, quotient
construction, membership and subsequent demands. Deferred comparisons are
revisited after quotient changes. Original child membership is checked first:
children may become reusable through result grounding. The retrieval-only
contrast still hydrates admitted rows because that mode marks every hit retrieved.

The sufficient context-inert predicate uses existing delivery conditions without
a new relevance cutoff. A comparison that cannot deliver for any result context
and proposes no new Union pair need not load that Outcome. A fresh full-Value
pair set per task/quotient records proposals only after original owner/Work/native
agreement and existing Candidate compilation succeed. Distinct pairs still need
original generators. Reversed equal pairs share a witness; hashes do not decide
equality. The original Match still supplies `LocalUnionOrigins`.

Single-source Work views now read original live membership and occurrence
orientation. Prepared-domain text/native flags cannot replace those witnesses
for deferred owners. Lean proves absence of automatic delivery for any result
context and coverage of suppressed Union proposals. ContractAudit checks the
production calls and retained roots. These local/fixed-pass properties are not
a full discovery-fixpoint refinement or complete indexed-recognition proof.

## Controlled checks

The helper compares every Handoff JSON field and body byte with unchanged
source-delta discovery. It supplies fixed numerical nominations/focus instead
of MiniLM. The reference links the already proved Matcher factoring.

All 32 small cases agree: shell and MCP-shaped invocations, children, persistent
aliases, positive local Unions and acknowledged histories. They include seven
completed and five blocking results and a nonempty local-Union case. All four
10k cases also agree: broad query, local-Union/retrieval-only modes and both
histories. In local-Union mode the counted owner callback drops from 9,969
distinct owners to nine. Bounded support/focus reads are not that same counter.

Elapsed time does not improve. Final controlled local-Union cases take
8,873/8,826 ms versus reference 8,485/8,467 ms. Retrieval-only takes
31,085/31,660 ms versus 28,576/28,701 ms. Earlier attempts, including the
deliberately canceled stale-executable comparison after a failed build, remain
recorded and are excluded from these final results.

first, without re-embedding or original writes. Each arm has fresh private

One pair per arm is not p99 or 50k qualification. No answering models or new
seeds were called. Total-token improvement and answer quality are unmeasured.
Product files, README results and releases remain unchanged under AGENTS.md.

## Remaining structural cost

The actual first candidate hook loads nine owners and projects 17 graph Outcomes,
but still reads all 9,969 original Work views and executes 19,929 Work admission
checks. Numerical cache acquisition costs 5,385 ms; encoder scoring is only
34,773 microseconds. Nested frequency/admission/read counters overlap and must
not be summed as independent costs.

Result deferral alone is insufficient. Next replace complete foreground Work
reconstruction with a complete canonical/affected-parent access path independent
of recommendation routing, and remove per-window acquisition before numerical
search. Synapses lead to selected original ranges through shared references.
Adding shards or another cache to the same complete scan would retain this cost.

Full fixpoint refinement, shared/federated lifecycle, cross-DB completeness,
routed additions, compaction, capture policy, 50k useful delivery and matched
token/quality qualification remain unfinished. A larger fixture cannot substitute
for changing the access path. This candidate is unadopted.
