# Compose independently prepared original owners

Isolated Lean component; **not integrated into ordinary warm/discovery and not
adopted**. It replaces the whole-tuple readiness condition rejected by the
[independent-cut counterexample](../owner-route/README.md). Original source
publication stays independently owned within one logical project. The ordinary
runtime `52e9dd63…` and previous component `98b7a651…` are unchanged.
The final checker is `eb923a3d…`; full hashes are in [result.json](result.json).

[SourceOwners](SourceOwners.lean) separates append-only structural identity from
per-source, exact-head liveness, original ordinal and both lexical posting
contracts. A digest nominates a bucket; full streamed original DAG comparison
preserves ordered Exact positions. Equality witnesses carry an immutable
original head and node rather than a source-array position. Retraction removes
only local liveness; reinsertion reuses the identity with its current ordinal.
These ids assert structural identity only, never advisory Work equivalence,
Union or completion authority.

Every source has its own PageMap index and captured reference. New roots use
checked publication deltas and already prepared lexical reverse terms. No
question-dependent tokenization or original-window reconstruction occurs in
the reader. Empty sources remain valid; missing individual preparation and
unavailable/backward deltas do not become successful empty domains. A request
may compose any permitted independently captured source cut; no composite
readiness name is consulted. Source permutation affects order without requiring
repreparation. The normal service must still validate complete inventory,
authorization and dependency closure before calling this component.

[SourceBitmap](SourceBitmap.lean) stores immutable 256-bit leaves and aligned
16-way paths, with exact cached cardinalities. Queries OR only membership bits,
counting shared owners once. Joins reuse equal persisted references, unchanged
children and request-local symmetric memo entries. Derived roots are never
written by a query. This is not an eviction-accounted service cache yet. A sparse
test at addresses through 1,000,001 checks block/height boundaries, removal,
union cardinality and repeated/reversed joins; it is **not a million-record
capacity test**.

The prototype uses one append-only dictionary/bitmap store for an authorized
identity namespace. Its short derived transactions are serialized; original
ACKs do not acquire that writer. This remaining serialization is **not** the
final 80-user preparation architecture, and no lock-free IO claim is made.
Per-source membership roots are independent, even though their immutable node
storage is currently shared. Splitting storage/writers must preserve stable
namespaced identity and exact unions; source-head combinations must never again
become readiness prerequisites.

## Validation boundary

The native [checker](../../../tools/research/SourceOwnerChecks.lean) compares
every owner, all ordered originals, exact Outcome/candidate counts and every
term of both lexical contracts against independent full `Federated.prepare` and
postings. It checks all four A0/B0, A1/B0, A1/B1, **A0/B1** cuts, foreign scope
and dictionary rejection, source permutation, genuinely empty versus unprepared
sources, final-copy retraction, stable reinsertion, structured noncandidate Work,
different Exact dependencies, pinned old cuts and fresh-process restoration.
An actual fork copied from A0 then appends a different record at A1's local
number. The identity-alias shortcut also compares the full original witness and
rejects this conflicting incarnation. The rejected attempt publishes no
completed source index; ordinary original heads remain unchanged.

The 80-source fixture also appends one original per independent owner in native
tasks while reading an old mixed cut, verifies every acknowledged source head,
then prepares each one-owner delta separately. This is a tiny local-filesystem
component test, not 80 network clients, 80 sustained preparations, a p99, a NAS
result, a numerical/normal-hook test or 98% necessary-evidence delivery.

The 80 tiny append tasks and concurrent retained-cut audit finish in 70 ms;
their whole append intervals overlap at most 40 at a time. This is not measured
overlap inside the durability critical section. Every acknowledged head is
retained. The subsequent 80 source-owner delta preparations are deliberately
serial: each reports one changed owner, takes 10–15 ms and sums to 1,003 ms.
That excludes lexical warm and is not an end-to-end preparation capacity claim.
Full reference audits take up to 8,625 ms; those include complete independent
materialization/comparison and are not a production speed baseline. Unchanged
preparation publishes no new root. Millisecond-zero observations are below the
clock's resolution.

The final native process peaks at 132,864 KiB aggregate RSS and fresh-process
reopening at 98,848 KiB. [Resources](resources.json) include every guard, the
3.5 GiB stop, 4 GiB hard ceiling and 2 GiB disk floor, with no trigger or owned
survivor. [Negative checks](negative-checks.json) retain earlier compile failures;
they are not successful validation. [Cleanup](cleanup.json) removes ended fixture
DBs/caches, raw logs and unused generated artifacts without archives, retaining
the current helper and required build inputs. Protected original hashes are
checked before and after deletion.

The focused bitmap theorems use only `propext` and `Quot.sound`. They prove OR
membership, idempotence and associativity; they do not prove dictionary IO,
source history authenticity, physical reclamation or concurrent publication.
Using a newer captured dictionary relies on the builder's append-only row
invariant and retained exact roots. Historical extent bounds alone are not a
general lineage or immutable-row proof.
