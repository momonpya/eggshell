# Completed authorization theorem

Status: an isolated, compiled candidate based on `cb05351`. Its production
functions and ContractAudit pass source-level checks. The executable and
native hooks have not been qualified, and the patch is not applied to the
main working implementation. Exact source, patch, checks and failed proof
attempts are recorded in `docs/candidates/completed-authority-review.json`.
This is the proved kernel component of Theorem 1. The complete production
claim still needs generator-origin and IO refinements.

## Theorem 1 statement

For a completed Work fragment in the executed extraction, assume the pinned
source IO binds every selected Match to an original Outcome owner. Then an
intrinsic, automatic Match witnesses the fragment's forward applicability and
local identity, and the projected Outcome either is that owner or retains
its relation as observation provenance. The selected prior Outcome remains
visible. Its owner-to-demand equality, and every executed selection equality
used for decomposition or child deduplication, follows from the current
persistent and local Union generators by reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity
and Semantic congruence, preserving the complete Exact footprint. The
completion witness is not a retrieval-only Match. A claim about every Union's
non-retrieval origin additionally requires the generator-ancestry premise.
Fresh delivery instantiates this kernel result after the source, freshness
and replay IO conditions below.

The candidate proves `Matcher.completed_original_authority` with an explicit
original-owner predicate. It supports sparse source IO without enumerating
the DB. `Matcher.completed_authority` supplies the kernel witness, and
`Matcher.extraction_comparisons_generated` covers all selected equality
premises. These are general proofs over the executed extraction. Their axiom
audit uses only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

The statement concerns authorization under the modeled policy. An Outcome's
meaning, an answer's correctness and a useful-history coverage guarantee
require separate evidence. `reusableOutcome` retains its structural meaning.

## Connection to the implemented producers

The final Discovery retains the persistent generators actually passed to the
controller and a proof that its quotient is generated by those generators
and its current local edges. The required proof has no default. Certified
view attachment uses the existing producer result; it does not add a second
equality build or an index fold to delivery.

The normal controller carries the certificate from the existing exact
ViewProvider, including acceleration and saved plans. The native terminal
path attaches generated authority to the existing Atom certificate. The
corresponding result-erasure lemmas preserve the original view and quotient.
The existing whole-controller provider-equivalence and terminal-equivalence
proofs compile with the added certificate.

ContractAudit checks both real producer paths. The renderer and receipt
functions accept a prepared extraction whose type equates it to
`Matcher.extraction discovery`; the audit checks that binding and its use.
Equality receipts take persistent generators directly from that Discovery,
so a caller cannot supply an unrelated list for offline classification.

The matching implementation preceding Discovery and the complete packet
renderer are byte-identical to the base. MiniLM, numerical/search providers,
source evidence checks, worker, hooks and graph store are unchanged. These
source comparisons do not replace native hook/body qualification.

## IO specification and remaining proof boundary

- The original-owner premise names owners from the pinned DB publication.
  Reader bytes, Value-to-node binding and the complete sparse-reader
  refinement are IO assumptions; the new theorem does not discharge them.
- `SourceEvidence.Authorization` requires valid recorded dependencies to
  agree with the observed current ledger. The existing
  `authorization_has_current_dependencies` theorem establishes equality with
  those recorded dependencies. Capture completeness and filesystem/crypto
  behavior remain IO boundaries. The kernel theorem alone cannot authorize
  delivery of stale source evidence.
- Memo replay can return a previously captured response without executing
  discovery again. `DemandMemo.dependenciesAgree` checks the pinned source
  heads or captured read dependencies. A complete replay argument must also
  connect that response to its original checked authorization; it is not
  established by the new extraction theorem.
- Source filtering, final packet fragments, stdout publication and host
  acknowledgement need their own binding to the selected witness. The
  existing owner/component receipts leave final-fragment causal ancestry
  incomplete.

Generated equivalence is distinct from the ancestry of a local Union's
proposal. The proof does not establish historical non-retrieval origins of
all persistent/local generators, scheduling, original-child roles or minimal
causal slices. Unknown ancestry stays unknown in Phase 3; an
all-literal extraction does not establish a congruence-free delivery.

## Verification scope

The candidate builds IncrementalQuotient, Matcher, the theorem module,
Handoff and ContractAudit. The kernel/matcher computational fixtures compile
with the new Discovery, including existing nonempty completed selections.
Test support and the full `TestMain.lean` elaborate after the receipt API
change. The final addition only strengthens the proof with the explicit
original-owner premise; its module and ContractAudit were built afterwards.
The full IO suite was not executed.

All resource guards stayed below their 3 GiB aggregate RSS stop. The peak
was 3,032,768 KiB. The proof checks used the separate 2 GiB disk reserve;
native preparation remains at 3 GiB and model prelaunch at 4 GiB. Available
disk space is below those latter gates, so the candidate remains isolated.
No answering model or new seed was requested. Answer/token variation is not
an adoption rejection gate.
